# Epuka — Business Overview

**One-liner:** Workflow-first preventive-healthcare logistics for Kenya.

## Problem
Preventive diagnostics in Kenya lack operational accountability. A blood panel touches a patient, an ops/logistics team, a lab and a medical reviewer — with no system ensuring each step happens correctly and on time.

## Solution
A role-based platform coordinating a diagnostic test end-to-end: patient booking → M-Pesa payment verification → home collection routing → partner-lab assignment → medical review → secure results release. Every role sees exactly the next action.

## Target market
Kenyan preventive-health market: patients, diagnostic/lab ops teams, partner labs, and medical admins.

## Competition
Traditional labs, home-sample-collection startups. Edge: operational accountability and a workflow spine rather than a booking form.

## Business model
Per-test diagnostics/logistics fees (M-Pesa); B2B deals with labs/clinics.

## Key metrics
North star: **diagnostic tests completed end-to-end**. KPIs: tests/week, turnaround time, result-release SLA, repeat patients.

## Moat
The operational workflow + lab network; trust in secure, accountable results handling.

## Risks
Regulatory/medical compliance and logistics reliability. Mitigate with strict role controls and audit trails.

## Status
Development. Stack: Next.js 14, Firestore, Firebase Auth, Leaflet, Netlify, M-Pesa.
